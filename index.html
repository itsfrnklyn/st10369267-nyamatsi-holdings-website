About Nyamatsi Holdings

Building quality solutions through professional construction, cleaning and consultancy services.

Who We Are

Nyamatsi Holdings is a multi-discipline professional construction firm serving municipalities, industries and communities through comprehensive construction, project management, architectural and surveying services.

Established in 2018, Nyamatsi Holdings was formed in response to opportunities within the general construction industry and consultancy sector.

The company is a 100% BEE company with a team of qualified and dedicated engineers, surveyors, technologists and other construction professionals.

Our team brings experience gained through working with established consulting companies and government sectors on major projects.

Nyamatsi Holdings provides construction and town and land surveying services throughout Southern Africa, both independently and in collaboration with other professional practices. The company is also actively involved in project and construction management.

🎯

Our Mission

Nyamatsi Holdings strives to build the best quality solutions while maintaining cost-effectiveness. We provide continuous support to assist our clients in achieving high-quality end products while maintaining a human touch. We focus on speed, continuous innovation and value-added services.

🌍

Our Vision

With a highly motivated team of young professionals, Nyamatsi Holdings aspires to become one of the top and most preferred construction firms in infrastructure development. Our aim is to achieve client satisfaction through collaboration, creativity, innovation and high-standard solutions.

OUR MANAGEMENT APPROACH

Professional Management and Quality Workmanship

Nyamatsi Holdings focuses on providing sound capital budgeting for projects and quality workmanship to meet client expectations. We aim to support the successful operation and maintenance of the projects we undertake.
